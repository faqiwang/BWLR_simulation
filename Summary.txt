This video demonstrates perception-guided terrain-adaptive control of a parallel bipedal wheel-legged robot, highlighting continuous mixed-terrain traversal and active hip-roll body shifting for obstacle avoidance with significantly reduced wheel lateral excursion, featuring:
(1) Perception and Policy Activation During Step Traversal and Height Adaptation;
(2) Terrain-Layout Generalization Changed Obstacle Spacing;
(3) Terrain-Layout Generalization Reordered Obstacles;
(4) Lateral-Obstacle Strategy Locked-Roll Steering Bypass; 
(5) Lateral-Obstacle Strategy Active Hip-Roll Body Shifting.
